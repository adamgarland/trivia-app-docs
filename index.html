Privacy Policy for Daily Trivia App

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Daily Trivia App ("the app," "we," "our," or "us") collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and how you can delete it.

Information We Collect

Your account

  • Email address and password: if you create an account with email. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google); we never see it.
  • Google account details: if you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with Firebase Authentication. We use your email to identify your account and don't display your name or photo in the app.
  • Username: the name you choose. It is shown to other players on the leaderboards.
  • Account ID: a random ID assigned to your account, used to connect your account to your game data and subscription.

Your gameplay

  • Points, streaks and leaderboard positions
  • Daily Quiz results (your score and which questions you got right)
  • Blitz scores
  • Quiz statistics such as your performance by topic

Custom quiz topics

When you create a custom quiz, the topic you type is sent to our server and to Google's Gemini AI service to write the questions. Please don't include personal information in a topic.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Premium, Apple processes the payment. We receive your subscription status and purchase history through RevenueCat, linked to your account ID. We never receive your payment card details.

Referrals

If you use or share a referral code, we store your referral code, who referred whom, and when. Entering someone's code (or having yours entered) also adds you to each other's friends list, which we store so you can see each other on the weekly Friends leaderboard. Only you can see your friends list, and you can remove a friend at any time. To stop one phone from claiming the same reward many times, we also store one-way hashed (SHA-256) versions of a random ID the app creates when installed and of Apple's identifier for vendor. The original identifiers can't be recovered from the hashes.

Usage analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used. It collects things like which screens and features you use, your device model, operating system and app version, an app-instance ID, and your approximate region (derived from your IP address). We don't link analytics to your account, username or email.

Stored only on your device

Your settings (such as light or dark mode), local statistics, custom quizzes and a cache of questions are stored on your device.

What we don't collect

  • We don't access your contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We don't use your device's advertising identifier, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

How We Use Your Information

  • To run your account and keep your progress across devices
  • To show leaderboards, streaks and statistics
  • To generate custom quizzes
  • To provide Premium and referral rewards
  • To prevent fraud and abuse
  • To fix problems and improve the app

Who We Share It With

We share information only with the service providers that run the app, and only for that purpose:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, our database, and analytics (Firebase privacy)
  • Google Gemini: writes questions for custom quiz topics (Google privacy policy)
  • RevenueCat: manages Premium subscriptions (RevenueCat privacy policy)
  • Apple: processes App Store payments (Apple privacy policy)
  • Vercel: hosts our server, which receives your requests, including your IP address (Vercel privacy policy)

Other players can see your username and points on the leaderboards. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently deletes:

  • your account and sign-in details
  • your username, points, streaks, leaderboard entries, Daily Quiz results and Blitz scores
  • your referral code, referral records and friends list (you're also removed from your friends' lists)
  • your subscription record at RevenueCat
  • the account data stored on your device

We keep the hashed device identifiers described under Referrals, with the link to your account removed, so the referral reward can't be claimed again from the same phone. Analytics data isn't linked to your account and expires under Google Analytics' retention settings.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Apple keeps its own record of your purchases.

If you can't access the app, email us and we'll delete your account for you.

Data Retention

  • Account and gameplay data are kept until you delete your account.
  • Data on your device is kept until you delete your account, delete the app, or clear its data.
  • Analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics for Firebase's retention settings.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with Google Firebase, which encrypts data at rest. Access to your account data is restricted by security rules so only you (and our server) can change it. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict how we use it. You can delete your account in the app at any time. For any other request, contact us using the details below. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app.
